A face, a post,

outlives the demo.

FaceChain encodes a face into a 128-d vector, searches Mastodon, Reddit, DuckDuckGo and Wikimedia for a live post carrying that same face, then anchors the finding on-chain as a hash. The post URL is not hardcoded. The match is not a filename comparison.
